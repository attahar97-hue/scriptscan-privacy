Privacy Policy for ScriptScan OCR

Effective date: October 15, 2026

1. Overview

ScriptScan OCR ("we", "our", or "the app") is a document scanner, handwritten notes digitizer, and OCR utility with optional AI features. It converts paper documents, notes, and receipts into editable text and PDF files.

This policy explains what information the app handles, who it is shared with, and what choices you have.

2. Information the app handles

  • Camera and photos: Used only to scan a document or import an image you choose. Scanning and edge detection are done with the Google ML Kit Document Scanner, which runs through Google Play Services. Scanned pages and extracted text are stored on your device. We do not upload them to our own servers, and we do not have servers that store your documents.
  • Text-to-speech: Reading text aloud uses your device's built-in Android text-to-speech engine.
  • Account data: The app does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, phone number, or email address.

3. AI features (Gemini API)

Summarize, Translate, Fix & Edit, and Math & Equation Solver are optional. They work only when you tap them. When you use one, the content you selected (text, and an image for features that read an image, such as the Math Solver) is sent over an encrypted HTTPS/TLS connection to Google's Gemini API, which processes it and returns the result.

This data is handled by Google under the Gemini API terms and Google's privacy policy. We do not control how Google processes it. Please do not use AI features on documents containing information you do not want to send to Google.

  • Gemini API Additional Terms of Service
  • Google Privacy Policy

Offline text extraction does not send anything to the internet.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob and Unity Ads)

The app is supported by ads. It uses Google AdMob and Unity Ads (through AdMob mediation) to show banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. A rewarded ad is a video you can choose to watch to unlock an AI feature.

This app uses third-party services such as Google AdMob and Unity Ads, which may collect device identifiers for ad delivery and personalization.

When ads are shown, these services may automatically collect and use the following information from your device:

  • Advertising ID (for example the Google Advertising ID) and other device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device model, operating system version, language, and network type
  • App and ad interactions, such as ads shown, viewed, or clicked, and rewarded ad completion
  • Diagnostic and performance data related to ad delivery

They use this information to serve and measure ads, limit repeated ads, prevent fraud and abuse, and, where permitted, personalize ads. We do not give these networks your scanned documents, images, or extracted text.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change this choice later if the app provides a privacy settings option. Ads in the app are not directed to children.

  • How Google uses data in ads
  • AdMob and Firebase data usage
  • Unity Privacy Policy

5. Your choices

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings: Settings > Google > Ads (the exact path can vary by device). You can also revoke camera or photo permissions at any time in your device's app settings.

6. Data deletion and your rights

  • Data in the app: Your scans, notes, and extracted text are stored only on your device. You can delete individual notes inside the app, or delete everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.
  • Data held by third parties: Google (Gemini, AdMob) and Unity process data under their own policies. To exercise access or deletion rights over that data, use the controls and contact links in their privacy policies listed above.
  • Questions or requests: Contact us at the email below and we will respond as soon as we can.

7. Security

Documents stay on your device, and communication with external services (Gemini API, ad networks) uses HTTPS/TLS encryption. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's privacy

ScriptScan OCR is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
